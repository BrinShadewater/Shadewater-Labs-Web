Privacy policy

SL Notebook is a private, single-user planner operated by Shadewater Labs. This policy describes its optional Oura connection. Connecting Oura authorizes SL Notebook to retrieve and save a rolling 30-day history for the account owner’s Health page. A connection previously used only for a temporary preview must be authorized again before saved sync begins.

SL Notebook requests Daily, Heart Rate, Workout, Session, and SpO2 permissions. Depending on the permissions you grant and the data your ring and account provide, this can include activity, cardiovascular age, readiness, resilience, sleep summaries and sleep sessions, blood oxygen, stress, heart-rate samples, rest-mode periods, guided and unguided sessions, sleep timing, estimated VO2 max, and workouts. Missing or unavailable categories are shown as unavailable or without data; SL Notebook does not invent measurements. It does not request your email address, profile information, or tags.

While connected, Hermes attempts a refresh every six hours and offers a manual sync control. Sync can be delayed by the ring, Oura, connectivity, permissions, or service availability. The Health page shows the last successful sync and any reported sync problems.

Oura records and connection tokens are encrypted at rest in a separate, access-restricted folder on Hermes. The encryption key is protected by Windows. Records older than the rolling 30-day window are removed by the service. Connection tokens are kept while needed for the connection. This Oura folder is excluded from SL Notebook’s application backups. Other health sources and manually entered records have separate storage behavior.

Oura history is available to the owner’s signed-in devices through the private Tailscale connection. The browser displays the retrieved records in memory; SL Notebook does not intentionally save Oura records or tokens to browser storage or an offline cache. Oura measurements and tokens are not included in application logs, Brine or Marg tools, the morning briefing, or AI services. Oura handles its own account and service data under the Oura Privacy Policy.

Choose Disconnect and delete Oura data in Health to stop this connection and remove its saved records and tokens from Hermes. This does not delete records in your Oura account. You may also withdraw SL Notebook’s authorization through your Oura account; use the Health disconnect-and-delete control to remove the copy already saved on Hermes without waiting for another sync. When a sync detects invalid or expired authorization, it removes the saved records and tokens and asks you to reconnect. For help with access or deletion, contact Shadewater Labs.
