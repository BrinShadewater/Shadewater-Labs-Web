Privacy policy

SL Notebook is a private, single-user planner operated by Shadewater Labs. This policy describes its optional Oura Readiness preview. Oura asks for your consent before SL Notebook uses the Daily permission to fetch up to 30 days of Readiness records.

The preview shows only the dates and Readiness scores in the browser that started the request. The server handles authorization and the results temporarily in memory. The result is available to retrieve once and the retrieval window expires five minutes after the authorization callback begins. SL Notebook does not save the scores, authorization tokens, or refresh tokens in browser storage, its database, or backups. Closing the preview clears the displayed scores.

The preview is accessible to the owner through a private Tailscale connection. SL Notebook uses the scores solely to show the preview and does not send them to Brine, Marg, the morning briefing, or AI services. Oura handles its own account and service data under the Oura Privacy Policy.

You can decline access at Oura, close the preview to discard the displayed scores, and withdraw authorization through your Oura account. For help with access, revocation, or a deletion request, contact Shadewater Labs.
