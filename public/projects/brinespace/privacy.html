Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026.

Game data on your device

BrineSpace is a single-player game. It does not require an account. The game stores your station saves, progress, settings, and local diagnostics on your device.

Optional bug reports

A diagnostic report can include your note, game logs, device and performance diagnostics, station saves and settings, a snapshot of the current station, and a screenshot. Choose Save report to keep the report on your device. Choose Save and send if you want to share it with Shadewater Labs. Please leave personal information out of your note.

When you send a report, the upload passes through Vercel and Resend delivers it as an email attachment to Shadewater Labs' support mailbox. We use reports to investigate bugs and respond to support requests. We do not use reports for advertising or tracking. Resend processes email data in the United States.

Retention and deletion

Reports received by email remain in the support mailbox until they are manually deleted. The game's upload service does not set an automatic expiry for the mailbox copy. Delivery providers can keep separate copies or service logs under their own retention schedules. Resend publishes its email and log retention periods, which depend on the plan.

You can stop sharing reports by choosing Save report or cancelling the report. To ask us to delete a report you already sent, email brinshadewater@gmail.com. Include the report filename or approximate submission date so we can find it. We can delete the mailbox copy and any copies we hold, and ask the delivery provider to remove its copy where applicable. Deleting a sent report does not remove the local file from your device.
